3. Advertising & App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

Meme Blitz is free to download and play. To sustain continuous game development, new puzzle packs, and server infrastructure, the game incorporates advertising provided by Google AdMob and authorized partner ad networks (AdMob Publisher ID: pub-6761529298065224).

Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) Prompt

In accordance with Apple's privacy guidelines, when you first launch Meme Blitz, iOS presents the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt asking whether you permit the app to track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies:

  • If you choose "Allow": Google AdMob and its advertising partners may use your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to serve personalized advertisements tailored to your interests and measure campaign efficacy.
  • If you choose "Ask App not to Track": Your IDFA is zeroed out and blocked. You will receive non-personalized (contextual) advertisements based solely on general app context, with zero cross-app tracking.

You can change or revoke your tracking choice at any time directly in your iPhone settings: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Meme Blitz.

Apple SKAdNetwork Attribution

Meme Blitz supports Apple's privacy-centric SKAdNetwork framework. SKAdNetwork allows advertisers to measure the success of ad campaigns without revealing the personal identity or cross-app browsing profile of any user.

4. Apple Game Center Leaderboards & Achievements

Meme Blitz integrates with Apple's Game Center framework to deliver social gameplay features:

  • Global and friends leaderboards for fastest solve times and total puzzles completed.
  • Milestone achievements and daily challenge streaks.

Game Center authentication and data storage are managed directly by Apple Inc. under the Apple Privacy Policy. Nova Labs does not possess access to your Game Center Apple ID password, and we do not store your Game Center profile credentials on third-party servers.

5. On-Device Game Data & Audio Preferences

All puzzle progress, unlocked difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, Hard), sound effect preferences, haptic feedback toggles, and personal records are saved strictly on your local device (using iOS UserDefaults and local app sandbox storage).

You can erase all locally stored records and game statistics at any time inside the app by opening Settings > Reset Progress Records.
